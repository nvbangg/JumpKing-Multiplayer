Privacy Policy for JumpKing Multiplayer

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JumpKing Multiplayer ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and protects your information when you access or play our online multiplayer game at https://jumpking-multiplayer.pages.dev/.

1. Information We Collect

When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0), we request your permission to receive basic profile information:

  • Google User ID (a unique identifier provided by Google)
  • Email address
  • Display Name (used to generate your in-game nickname)

We do not collect or store your Google password, contacts, or any other personal Google data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity and log you into the game.
  • Create and maintain your game profile and save progress.
  • Display your nickname on the public game leaderboard and match results.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties or advertisers. All data is strictly utilized for the gameplay functionalities of JumpKing Multiplayer.

4. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All web communications are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • User sessions are handled with secure, HttpOnly session cookies.
  • Game records and identifiers are stored in a secured, private database.

5. Data Deletion and User Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated game data at any time. To request deletion, please contact us at quangxuan1301@gmail.com. We will process your request promptly.
